`/reseed` guides a context-window reduction when work is mid-flight via **seed-and-clear**: it authors a durable seed — pointers to the on-disk artifacts that hold the state, plus the volatile working-state and conversation-only operating-decisions that have no durable home (each grant carried with its scope *and* live status, never duplicating what's on disk) — then hands you the exact `/clear`-and-revive follow-up. There is no decision step: in real use seed-and-clear is the right call whenever work is mid-flight. For a cheap summarize where little is at stake, use `/compact` directly. See `docs/skill-reference.md` for details.

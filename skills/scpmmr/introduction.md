`/scpmmr` is `/scpmr` then `/mmr`: commit, push, open and precheck the PR/MR, then merge it with a squash commit and branch deletion. Typed after a precheck stop, it is your merge call and goes straight to the merge. Typed before anything has been prechecked, it takes the work to the precheck gate, which still stops for your merge call unless an exception applies (kahuna sandbox, armed autochain, standing merge grant, lead-agent grant). For the full workflow walkthrough, run `/ccwork tour workflow`.

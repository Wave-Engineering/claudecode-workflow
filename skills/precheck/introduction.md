`/precheck` is the mandatory PR/MR gate, the only way into a release branch. Commits and pushes to your working branch are free; precheck reviews the pushed commits, reads CI, checks the issue and dependencies, and on a pass posts a `precheck` status on the commit and marks the PR/MR ready. Then it stops for your merge call. After the PR/MR is open, a later push needs only `/precheck rearm`: a review of the new commits and a green pipeline. This is the single most important workflow skill: nothing lands unreviewed. For the full workflow, run `/ccwork tour workflow`.

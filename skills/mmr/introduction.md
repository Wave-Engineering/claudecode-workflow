`/mmr` merges a PR/MR with a squash commit and deletes the source branch afterward. It is the merge gate: it needs a `precheck` pass on the PR/MR's head (a push since then is rearmed with a quick review of only the new commits), CI green, and your merge call or a mechanical exception. It works with both GitHub PRs and GitLab MRs. For the workflow walkthrough, run `/ccwork tour workflow`.
